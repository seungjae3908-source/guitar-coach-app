# Focus AI v1 physical-device checklist

1. Open the existing app and confirm the floating `AI 집중` button appears.
2. Open the AI focus screen and switch through all five modes.
3. Set 60 BPM, 30 seconds, normal sensitivity.
4. Grant microphone permission.
5. Start a session and verify the three-second countdown.
6. Verify four visual beat dots and haptic beats while running.
7. Play clearly and confirm the microphone level bar and detected-hit counter react.
8. Stop after at least five seconds and confirm a score and up to two coaching messages appear.
9. Confirm Korean speech reads the first coaching message when voice feedback is enabled.
10. Close and reopen the app and confirm focus session history remains.
11. Confirm camera recording and gallery saving still work.

A physical-device pass is not claimed until the user completes these checks.
